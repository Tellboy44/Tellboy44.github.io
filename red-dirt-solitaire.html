Privacy Policy

Red Dirt Solitaire · Terence Burley · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Red Dirt Solitaire handles information. In short: no ads, no tracking, no account, and no personal data collected by Terence Burley.

Information used in the game

Your game progress, statistics, Daily Deal streak, and settings (such as music, sounds, table colour and card backs) are stored only on your device. The game does not require an account, does not show advertising or use advertising identifiers, does not include third-party analytics, and does not request location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

The one-off unlock

Red Dirt Solitaire includes some free hands, then offers a single one-off in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. The purchase is handled entirely by the store you installed the game from (Google Play, the Amazon Appstore or the Microsoft Store), including payment. The game only receives confirmation from that store that the unlock has been bought, so it can unlock the game and restore it if you reinstall. Terence Burley does not receive your payment details, and the game does not send purchase information to any server run by Terence Burley.

Who processes information

  • Google Play: handles Android purchases from Google Play. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Amazon Appstore: handles purchases on Amazon Fire and other Amazon Appstore devices. See Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Microsoft Store: handles Windows purchases and answers the game's licence check. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this policy page and may process ordinary website request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can remove all game progress and settings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Purchase records are kept by the store you bought from under its own privacy terms.

Children's privacy

Red Dirt Solitaire is a general-audience card game. It does not ask anyone to provide names, email addresses or other contact details to play.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The latest version and its date will always be published on this page.
